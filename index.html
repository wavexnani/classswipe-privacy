ClassSwipe

Privacy Policy

ClassSwipe helps college students track attendance, timetable subjects, reminders, and attendance health. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is protected.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Quick summary Data we handle Permissions Third-party services Your rights Contact

1. Quick Summary

  • ClassSwipe stores your core attendance and timetable data on your device.
  • Timetable image text recognition is processed on device using ML Kit. The app does not upload your timetable image for OCR.
  • Analytics and crash reporting are consent-gated and are not initialized until you agree in the app.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can erase app data and revoke analytics consent from the app.

2. Who We Are

The data fiduciary/controller for ClassSwipe is wavexnani. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact wavexnani@gmail.com.

3. Data We Handle

Attendance data Subject names, subject codes, present counts, absent counts, edit history, attendance health, bunk buffer, and recovery calculations.
Timetable data Timetable images you select, extracted subject text, timetable slots, and locally saved timetable history.
QR transfer data Attendance or timetable payloads that you choose to share or import through QR features. QR sharing is user-controlled.
Device-scoped identifiers Random app-generated IDs stored in secure device storage. These help keep local records consistent and support sync-related logic if enabled.
Analytics events Consent-gated product usage events, such as onboarding completion or feature usage. These events are used to improve the app.
Crash diagnostics Consent-gated crash and error reports used to diagnose stability issues. Reports may include technical device, OS, app version, and error context.

ClassSwipe does not require your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, or payment information to track attendance.

4. How We Use Data

  • To calculate attendance percentage, safe/risk status, bunk buffer, and recovery classes.
  • To save your subjects, timetable, QR imports, and attendance history on your device.
  • To send optional attendance reminders and critical attendance alerts.
  • To improve app quality, reliability, and feature design when you consent to telemetry.
  • To prevent accidental corruption and maintain app data integrity.

5. Storage and Retention

Attendance, timetable, and app preference data is stored locally on your device using SQLite, SecureStore, and MMKV. Local data remains until you delete it, reset the app, clear app storage, or uninstall ClassSwipe.

Analytics and crash data, when enabled by consent, may be retained by the relevant third-party provider according to that provider's retention policy.

6. App Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes and, where available, select or capture timetable images.
  • Photos or media: used when you choose a timetable image from your device.
  • Notifications: used only if you enable attendance reminders or alerts.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Disabling a permission may limit the related feature but will not stop core attendance tracking.

7. Consent

ClassSwipe uses a consent gate during onboarding. Analytics and crash diagnostics are initialized only after consent is recorded. You can revoke analytics consent in the app's Privacy & Data settings. Revoking consent stops telemetry collection and clears the consent timestamp.

8. Third-Party Services

ClassSwipe may use the following service providers:

  • PostHog: product analytics, only after consent.
  • Sentry: crash and error diagnostics, only after consent and when configured.
  • Google ML Kit: on-device text recognition for timetable images.
  • Supabase: optional backend or sync-related features if enabled in the app build.
  • Expo services: build, notification, and app infrastructure services used by the app ecosystem.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We only use providers to operate, secure, diagnose, or improve ClassSwipe.

9. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to operate app features, comply with law, protect rights and security, or process data that you intentionally share through features such as QR transfer.

10. Children's Privacy

ClassSwipe is intended for college students aged 18 and above. The onboarding age gate does not store your date of birth. If you believe a minor has provided personal data through ClassSwipe, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 where applicable, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, or withdraw consent for personal data.

  • Use in-app reset controls to erase local ClassSwipe data.
  • Use Privacy & Data settings to change analytics consent.
  • Use device settings to manage camera, media, and notification permissions.
  • Email us for privacy requests that cannot be completed in the app.

12. Security

ClassSwipe uses local storage, secure device storage for sensitive identifiers and consent values, encrypted MMKV where configured, and release hardening such as Android backup restrictions and code shrinking. No app or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as ClassSwipe changes or as legal requirements evolve. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

If a change materially affects how ClassSwipe handles your data, we will take reasonable steps to notify users through the app or store listing where appropriate.
