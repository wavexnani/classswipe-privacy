Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 19, 2026

Introduction

Proxi is an attendance tracking app for Indian college students. We are committed to protecting your privacy under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Data Controller

wavexnani, reachable at wavexnani@gmail.com.

What We Collect

  • Attendance records (subject names, present/absent counts) — stored locally on device only.
  • Timetable data (class schedule extracted from uploaded image) — stored locally on device only.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (screen views and feature usage via PostHog) — no personal identifiers.
  • Device identifier (a random ID for sync conflict resolution) — not linked to identity.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any personally identifiable information.

Why We Collect It

We collect this data to calculate attendance percentage, bunk buffer, and recovery classes. Anonymous analytics help improve the app. All computation happens on-device.

Third-Party Services

PostHog is used for anonymous analytics. Read PostHog Privacy Policy here: https://posthog.com/privacy. We do not share your data with any other third party.

Data Storage

All attendance and timetable data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device. Anonymous analytics events are sent to PostHog servers.

Your Rights Under DPDP Act, 2023

  • Right to access your personal data processed through the app.
  • Right to correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure using the Erase All Data option in app Settings.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time from app Settings.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Age verification is enforced during onboarding.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app means acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, email wavexnani@gmail.com.
